# Research brief — short-form growth for "חתול בהמתנה" (17.10.2026)

Confidence: [O] official/executive quote · [S] trade press citing official · [D] third-party dataset · [L] creator lore. Turned into rules in SERIES_BIBLE §O.

## Instagram Reels
- Top signals: watch time, likes, sends. Mosseri (Jan 2025): sends matter most for reaching non-followers [O]. "Sends per reach" named a top signal [O 2024]. Under 100 followers, nearly all reach is non-followers → write for sends.
- Watch time counts % and seconds; longer reels not penalized for lower completion [S]. Length by story, not formula.
- Insights show "Skip rate" (left in first 3 s) since Aug 2025 [S]; Metricool IGRE28 is its complement.
- Originality: copies replaced by originals; accounts mostly reposting lose recommendations (30-day window from Apr 2026) [O/S]; other apps' watermarks block recommendation, own logo OK [O]; engagement bait blocks recommendation [O]; reported Dec 2025 de-prioritization of "templated/automated" content [S, unverified].
- AI labels: no official evidence of reach cut [O]; authenticity becoming "scarce" (Mosseri) [S].
- Trial Reels [O]: shown to non-followers first, metrics after ~24 h, optional auto-share if it performs in 72 h. Change one variable; compare trials with trials; judge on skip rate then avg watch.
- Frequency: Buffer 2.1M posts — +12% reach/post at 3–5/wk, +24% at 10+/wk vs 1–2; weeks with no posts hurt [D].
- Hashtags don't boost reach; keywords do [S]. Meta AI reel translation has no Hebrew (Apr 2026) [S].

## TikTok
- Follower count not a direct ranking factor; completion is strong [O].
- AI labels: required for realistic AI; auto-labels via C2PA; reportedly no distribution effect when compliant [S]; viewer "see less AI" slider test (Nov 2025) [S]; account-level AI-spam detection tests (Jul 2026, aimed at politics/finance/medical) [O].
- Frequency: Buffer 11.4M posts — median flat (~500); top-decile grows with volume ("posting more = more lottery tickets") [D].
- Playlists need ~10k followers [L]. Video replies to comments exist [O]. Keywords spoken + on screen + caption [L].

## YouTube Shorts
- Since 31.3.2025 every start/replay counts as a view; old metric = "engaged views" [O via PPC Land] → raw Shorts views inflated vs Reels/TikTok.
- Shorts → long-form recommendations on the same channel [O]; Shorts can link a related video [S].
- "Inauthentic content" (15.7.2025) affects monetization, not visibility [S]. Animated/unrealistic content needs no AI disclosure [O].
- Shorts keep gaining views up to ~30 days [D] → judge at day 7 and day 28.

## Facebook
- Israel: ~8.2M Facebook users (85%) vs ~5.8M Instagram (60%) (NapoleonCat, Sep 2026) [D]. Original Reels' views roughly doubled H2 2025 vs H2 2024 [S].

## Series formats
- Fruit Love Island (TikTok, Mar 2026, AI): ~300M views in 9 days; daily numbered episodes, audience voting, comments read in-episode; ended after mass reports and AI hostility [S].
- AI cat soap operas (2024): recurring character + melodrama, fast copycats [S].

## Engagement
- Keyword comment prompts allowed if the engagement is genuine (Instagram, Jun 2024) [S]. Comment-to-DM: one private reply per comment within 7 days [S].
- "Follow so the cat moves forward" may read as follow-bait → keep the queue in the story, explanation in pinned comment and bio [L/O-adjacent].

## Measuring a tiny account (computed)
- 1,700 views at 47% hold → ±2.4 pp. ~200 views → ±7 pp; hook differences < 10 pp = noise. 2 shares / 1,700 → true send rate 0.01–0.4%.
- Use medians; decide hook style after 5–8 paired trials, format after 10–14 posts; Reels/TikTok at 72 h, Shorts at day 7 and 28.

## Israel specifics
